## Mapping the Philippine Restaurant Landscape: Food-Type Diversity, Customer Engagement, and the Distribution of Restaurant Listings Across Selected Cities
### General Objective
To analyze the Philippine restaurant landscape across selected cities by examining food-type diversity, customer engagement, and the distribution of restaurant listings using a dataset of Foodpanda restaurant listings collected in 2025.
### Specific Objectives
1. To assess, clean, and prepare the dataset by investigating missing FoodType values, inconsistent text formatting, repeated restaurant identifiers, unrated listings, and unusual reviewer counts, while documenting and justifying every cleaning decision.

2. To quantify and compare food-type diversity across selected cities by examining the number of unique FoodType categories and their proportional representation among restaurant listings.

3. To assess customer engagement and feedback across cities and food types by analyzing reviewer counts and average ratings among rated restaurant listings.

4. To examine the distribution and concentration of restaurant listings across selected cities and food types by comparing listing counts, proportions, and city-by-food-type summaries.

5. To explore the relationships among food-type diversity, restaurant-listing distribution, reviewer activity, and average ratings using relevant descriptive statistics, group comparisons, correlation analysis, and a basic inferential method where appropriate, without interpreting association as causation.

6. To communicate the findings clearly through 4–6 appropriate, accurately labeled, honestly scaled, and captioned visualizations, supported by plain-language interpretations and a discussion of the dataset’s limitations.
